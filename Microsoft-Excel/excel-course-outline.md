## Course concept

This course teaches Excel through separate real-world cases involving online stores, digital product creators, and forex traders. Students act as junior analysts, using Excel to study other people’s business or trading data and generate useful insights.

## Modules

1. Introduction to Excel and real-world data  
2. Data entry, formatting, and organization  
3. Basic Excel formulas and calculations  
4. Sorting, filtering, and data cleaning  
5. Summarizing data with tables and PivotTables  
6. Creating charts and visual reports  
7. Interpreting results and identifying trends  
8. Making recommendations from data  
9. Excel versus Access for data analysis  
10. Final case-study analysis  

## Overall learning outcomes

By the end of the course, students should be able to:

- Organize raw data into a clear Excel worksheet.
- Use basic formulas to calculate useful results.
- Clean, sort, filter, and summarize data.
- Create simple charts and reports.
- Identify patterns, trends, and performance differences.
- Use Excel to support practical business or trading decisions.
- Explain when Excel is more suitable than Access.
- Present data-based recommendations clearly.

## Connection to real-world cases

The course questions should always connect Excel skills to realistic problems, such as:

- What is performing well?
- What is underperforming?
- Where are the biggest opportunities?
- What patterns can be seen in the data?
- What decision could improve results?
- How reliable is the conclusion?

The online store, digital product, and forex cases provide context for these questions. Students learn Excel not by memorizing isolated functions, but by using it to investigate real-world situations and make evidence-based recommendations.
